# The Fibonacci numbers

The Fibonacci numbers were introduced by the Italian mathematician Leonardo of Pisa, known as *Fibonacci*, in the 13th century. They form a sequence where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones:

**0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ...**

Mathematically:

[
\begin{cases}
F(0) = 0, \
F(1) = 1, \
F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2), & \text{ha } n \ge 2
\end{cases}
]

Fibonacci described this sequence while studying the growth of rabbit populations.
