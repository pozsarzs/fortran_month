# The Fibonacci numbers

The Fibonacci numbers were introduced by the Italian mathematician Leonardo of Pisa, known as *Fibonacci*, in the 13th century. They form a sequence where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones: 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ...

Mathematically:

$$
F(0) = 0; F(1) = 1; F(n) = F(n−1) + F(n−2)
$$

Fibonacci described this sequence while studying the growth of rabbit populations.
